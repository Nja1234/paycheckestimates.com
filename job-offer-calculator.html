How to Compare Job Offers After Taxes

A higher salary doesn't always mean more money in your pocket. State income tax rates vary dramatically — from 0% in Texas and Florida to over 13% in California. Our free job offer comparison calculator shows the real after-tax difference between any two offers.

💰
Check State Taxes First
A $10,000 salary difference can vanish entirely when comparing a high-tax state vs a no-tax state. Always compare after-tax, not gross salary.
🏥
Value Benefits Properly
Employer health insurance can be worth $8,000–$15,000/year. A 401(k) match of 4% on $80K is $3,200/year in free money.
🏙️
Factor in Cost of Living
$80,000 in Austin goes much further than $80,000 in San Francisco. Use a cost of living calculator alongside this tool.
📈
Think Long-Term
A lower starting salary at a high-growth company may be worth more than a higher salary with no growth potential.
