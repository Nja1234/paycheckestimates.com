How Much of Your Raise Do You Actually Keep?

When you get a raise, you don't keep 100% of it. Federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare all take a share. The percentage you keep depends on your tax bracket and state. Our free salary raise calculator shows you the exact after-tax amount for your specific situation.

Does a Raise Push You Into a Higher Tax Bracket?

Even if a raise pushes you into a higher tax bracket, only the income above the threshold is taxed at the higher rate. The US uses a progressive tax system — your entire salary is never taxed at your top bracket rate. A raise always results in higher take-home pay, never lower.
