Frequently Asked Questions

States with no income tax give the highest take-home pay: Texas, Florida, Nevada, Washington, Wyoming, South Dakota, Tennessee, New Hampshire, and Alaska. Among these, Texas and Florida are the most popular for relocating high earners due to their large job markets.
On a $80,000 salary, moving from California to Texas saves approximately $5,000–$7,000 per year in state income tax alone. California's effective rate for this income level is around 6–7%, while Texas has no income tax. Use the comparison tool above for your exact salary.
Generally, you pay state income tax in the state where you work. If you live in one state and work in another, you may owe taxes in both states (though tax credits usually prevent double taxation). Remote workers typically pay taxes based on where they perform their work. Check with a tax professional for your specific situation.
