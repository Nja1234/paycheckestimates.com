  - .com: **37 (70%)**
  - .shop: **5 (9%)**
  - .site: **3 (6%)**
  - other: **8 (15%)**
  - .gov/.edu: **0**
- Visible countries include Moldova (4), United States (1), and China (1).
- The top-anchor cloud contains suspicious/non-brand phrases such as `high quality ... online cheap` and multiple `guest posting` phrases.
- Semrush shows only **3 referring domains** to the homepage and **1** to the privacy policy in the visible Top Pages card; the Pennsylvania page shows no direct referring domains in that card.

### Meaning

The backlink count looks much better than the backlink quality. A large number of zero/very-low authority domains with odd anchors is unlikely to provide the ranking lift needed for a finance calculator site. Do not buy more automated, guest-post-network, directory, `.shop`, or `.site` links just to increase the referring-domain count.

Random spam links are common and Google often ignores them. A disavow is not the first move unless these links were deliberately built in volume, you have a manual action, or you have clear evidence of manipulative link building that cannot be removed.

## What I would do in the next 7 days

### Priority 1 — Deploy the Pennsylvania v2 page immediately

This is the page Google is already testing for the broadest cluster. Improving the existing URL is much faster than creating a new URL and waiting for it to establish relevance.

### Priority 2 — Request recrawl, then stop changing the page daily

After deployment:

- Submit `/states/pennsylvania.html` in Google Search Console URL Inspection.
- Resubmit the sitemap if needed.
- Leave the title/H1 stable for at least several days so ranking movement can be interpreted.

### Priority 3 — Build 3–5 real links, not 50 weak links

The immediate off-page goal is a few contextual links from real US sites with actual traffic and relevant audiences. Best themes:

- personal finance / salary / career websites,
- Pennsylvania employment or relocation resources,
- HR/payroll/accounting resources,
- tax-preparation or bookkeeping blogs,
- data/calculator resource roundups that genuinely evaluate the tool.

Link directly to `/states/pennsylvania.html` where context is Pennsylvania/payroll-specific. Use natural anchors such as the brand name, URL, `Pennsylvania paycheck calculator`, or descriptive sentence anchors—not repeated exact-match anchors.
